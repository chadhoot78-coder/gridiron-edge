How the number is built

Team rating. Every game played this season counts: point margin (home-field removed), yards-per-play margin, and turnover margin, adjusted for who the opponent was. Ratings are shrunk toward average when the sample is small — 70% after one game, about 40% after four, 20% after ten — so the stats earn trust as the season goes.

Learning. After each completed week the app replays the season: for every past week it rebuilds ratings from only the weeks before, predicts that week, and scores each stats/market weight and shrink speed against the real margins. The Auto button uses whatever backtested best. It needs at least one week of games after Week 1 to have anything to learn from.

Line tracking. The first spread the app sees for a game is saved as the opener. ▲ means the line has moved toward the pick (the market agrees), ▼ means it's moved away. Graded picks are saved so the record carries week to week.

Model line. Home rating − away rating + 1.5 pts home field.

Blend. The slider mixes the model line with the market line. Default is 35% stats / 65% market, because the line already prices injuries, weather, and matchups. Nudge it toward stats as the season goes on.

Probability. Expected margin ÷ 13.5 (the historical std. dev. of NFL margins) through a normal curve. ATS pick. Every game gets a side: whichever team the blended margin favors against the number. Edges under 1.5 points are marked coin flips. Once a game is final the pick grades W/L and the record at the top updates.
